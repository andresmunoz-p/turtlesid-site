Turtles iD identifies individual sea turtles from a photo of the scales on their head, on a phone in the field or a laptop at the station, with no internet connection. WhyPor qué

The problemEl problema

Following individual turtles for decades means tags that hurt and get lost, or hours comparing photos by eye as the catalog grows.Seguir a cada tortuga durante décadas exige marcas invasivas que se pierden, u horas comparando fotos a ojo a medida que crece el catálogo.

The ideaLa idea

The scales on a turtle's head form a unique pattern that never changes. A photo is enough, and it doesn't touch the animal.Las escamas de la cabeza forman un patrón único que no cambia en toda la vida. Basta una foto, sin tocar al animal.

The resultEl resultado

The app suggests the closest turtles in seconds, offline, and a researcher confirms. It never decides alone.La app sugiere las tortugas más parecidas en segundos, sin conexión, y un investigador confirma. Nunca decide sola.

How it worksCómo funciona

Guided capture checks sharpness, light and motion, and shoots by itself.La captura guiada revisa nitidez, luz y movimiento, y dispara sola.
A model finds the head, crops it and tells left from right.Un modelo localiza la cabeza, la recorta y distingue izquierda y derecha.
The species is suggested; the researcher can choose it.Se sugiere la especie; el investigador puede elegirla.
A 512-number "fingerprint" is compared with every catalog photo of that side.Una "huella" de 512 números se compara con cada foto del catálogo de ese lado.
"Very likely", "possible" or "no confident match": the researcher decides."Muy probable", "posible" o "sin coincidencia segura": decide el investigador.
